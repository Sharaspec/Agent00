## 1. Activity guidelines and defaults
- Adults 18–64 (WHO 2020, US PAG 2nd ed.): 150–300 min/week moderate OR 75–150 vigorous (1 vigorous min ≈ 2 moderate); muscle-strengthening all major groups ≥2 days/week; any amount counts; sit less. 65+: add balance work (3+ days if poor mobility).
- Defaults: steps 8,000/day (Paluch 2022, Lancet Public Health: benefit levels off 6–8k for 60+, 8–10k under 60); 30 active min/day (~150/week); 2 strength sessions/week; 8 cups water as a starter (National Academies AI total water ≈3.7 L men / 2.7 L women, ~20% from food); sleep 7+ h.

## 2. Workout templates (K, ACSM-style)
General: 5–10 min warm-up; beginners stop 2–3 reps short of failure; progress reps then load (2–10%); rest 60–90 s (hypertrophy), 2–3 min heavy compounds.

Full-body gym (2–3×/wk)
- Beginner: goblet squat; DB bench or push-up; seated cable row; DB Romanian deadlift; lat pulldown; plank — 2×10–12 (plank 3×20–30 s).
- Intermediate A/B: A back squat, bench, barbell row, plank; B deadlift, overhead press, pull-up/pulldown, walking lunge — 3×6–10 (lunge 3×10/leg).
- Advanced 3-day: D1 squat 5×5, bench 5×5, row 4×6; D2 front squat 4×8, incline DB press 4×10, pull-up 4×AMRAP, RDL 3×10; D3 deadlift 5×3, OHP 4×6, weighted chin-up 4×6, Bulgarian split squat 3×8.

Bodyweight at home
- Beginner: chair squat ×10; wall/incline push-up ×8; glute bridge ×12; bird-dog ×6/side; dead bug ×6/side; knee plank 20 s — 2–3 rounds, 60 s rest.
- Intermediate: squat ×15; push-up ×10; reverse lunge ×10/leg; table/towel row ×10; side plank 30 s/side; mountain climbers 30 s — 3–4 rounds.
- Advanced: jump squat ×15; decline/archer push-up ×8/side; Bulgarian split squat ×12/leg; pike push-up ×10; single-leg RDL ×10/leg; hollow hold 40 s; burpee ×10 — 4–5 rounds, 45 s rest.

Push/Pull/Legs (intermediate 3 days; advanced 6 days)
- Push: bench 4×6–8; OHP 3×8–10; incline DB press 3×10; lateral raise 3×12–15; triceps pushdown 3×12; overhead triceps ext 2×12.
- Pull: deadlift/rack pull 3×5; pull-up/pulldown 4×8; row 3×10; face pull 3×15; DB curl 3×12; hammer curl 2×12.
- Legs: back squat 4×6–8; RDL 3×8–10; leg press 3×12; walking lunge 3×10/leg; leg curl 3×12; calf raise 4×12–15; hanging knee raise 3×12.

HIIT (1–3×/wk, not back-to-back, 20–30 min total)
- Beginner: 20 s on / 40 s off × 8–10, low impact: march/high knees, squat, step-back lunge, incline push-up, skater step.
- Intermediate: 30/30 × 12–16 or Tabata 20/10 × 8 ×2 blocks: jumping jacks, jump squats, push-ups, mountain climbers, speed skaters.
- Advanced: 40/20 × 16–20 or EMOM 20: burpees, jump lunges, tuck jumps, plank jacks, sprints (30 s all-out / 90 s easy × 6–10).

Couch to 5K (NHS structure; 3 runs/wk; 5 min walk warm-up and cool-down)
W1 run 60 s/walk 90 s ×8 · W2 run 90 s/walk 2 min ×6 · W3 2×(run 90 s, walk 90 s, run 3, walk 3) · W4 run 3, walk 1.5, run 5, walk 2.5, run 3, walk 1.5, run 5 · W5 R1 3×(run 5, walk 3) R2 run 8, walk 5, run 8 R3 run 20 · W6 R1 run 5, walk 3, run 8, walk 3, run 5 R2 run 10, walk 3, run 10 R3 run 25 · W7 25 min ×3 · W8 28 min ×3 · W9 30 min ×3 (≈5 km).
- Intermediate 5K→10K, 4 runs/wk: easy 30–40; tempo 10 easy + 15–20 comfortably hard + 10 easy; intervals 6×400 m / 200 m jog → 5×1 km; long run 45–70 (≤10%/wk).
- Advanced 5–6 runs/wk: ~80% easy; threshold 3×10 min; VO2 5×1000 m at 5K pace (2–3 min jog); long run 75–100 min.

Walking
- Beginner: 10–15 min easy, 5 days/wk, +5 min/wk to 30.
- Intermediate: 30–45 min brisk (≈100+ steps/min, 3–4 mph), 5 days/wk, one hill route/wk.
- Advanced: 45–60 min power walk or intervals (3 fast / 2 moderate × 6); weekly 90+ min hike; optional rucking 5–10 kg.

Mobility (holds 10–30 s, ~60 s per muscle group, 2–3+ days/wk)
- Beginner 10 min: neck rolls ×5/side; shoulder rolls ×10; cat-cow ×8; child's pose 30 s; standing hamstring 30 s/side; half-kneel hip flexor 30 s/side; calf wall 30 s/side.
- Intermediate 15 min: world's greatest stretch ×5/side; 90/90 hip switches ×8; thoracic open-book ×8/side; down dog → cobra ×8; deep squat hold 3×30 s; pigeon 45 s/side; doorway pec 30 s/side.
- Advanced 20–25 min: CARs neck/shoulders/hips/ankles ×3–5; Cossack squat ×8/side; light Jefferson curl ×8; couch stretch 60 s/side; active straight-leg raise ×10/side; dead hang 3×30 s; sun salutation ×5.

## 4. Healthy eating habits
1. Plate method (Harvard Healthy Eating Plate): ½ veg & fruit (potatoes don't count), ¼ whole grains, ¼ healthy protein (fish, beans, nuts, poultry, eggs; limit red meat, avoid processed meat); healthy oils; water/tea/coffee, skip sugary drinks, milk 1–2/day.
2. Protein: RDA 0.8 g/kg/day; 65+ 1.0–1.2 g/kg (PROT-AGE); active/strength 1.4–2.0 g/kg (ISSN, K); ~20–40 g per meal spread over the day. Kidney disease: ask a clinician.
3. Fiber: ~14 g per 1,000 kcal ≈ 25 g women / 31–38 g men (K); most people get half. Beans, lentils, whole grains, veg, fruit, nuts, seeds; increase gradually with water.
4. Hydration: total ≈3.7 L men / 2.7 L women from drinks + food; pale-yellow urine check; more in heat/exercise.
5. Ultra-processed food: BMJ 2024 umbrella review (45 meta-analyses, ~10M people) linked higher UPF intake to 32 adverse outcomes (observational). Eat mostly foods with short, recognizable ingredient lists; cook at home more.
6. Other (WHO/DGA, K): free sugars <10% kcal; sodium <2,300 mg (WHO <2,000); sat fat <10% kcal; ≥5 portions (400 g) fruit & veg; fish ~2×/week; limit alcohol.

## 7. Daily ideas (60)
Movement: 10-min walk after lunch · 3 rounds of 10 squats, 10 push-ups, 30 s plank · take the stairs all day · walking meeting or call · 15-min kitchen dance party · bike or walk to an errand · new 20-min yoga video · 5-min stretch before bed · move 2 min every hour · walk somewhere new · 7-min bodyweight circuit · brush teeth on one leg.
Eating: half plate veg at dinner · a veg you haven't had in a month · glass of water before each meal · swap a sugary drink for sparkling water · prep tomorrow's lunch tonight · add beans or lentils to a meal · one screen-free slow meal · leftover grain bowl · protein at breakfast · fruit or nuts instead of packaged snacks · cook one meal from scratch · read 3 ingredient lists in your pantry.
Mind: 5 min box breathing (4-4-4-4) · 3 things you're grateful for · 10-min body scan · mindful walk, notice 5 sounds · no phone first 30 min after waking · 5-min feelings journal · 10 min outside in daylight.
Social: message a friend you haven't talked to lately · invite someone for a walk · share a meal with family or friends · send a thank-you note · join a class, club or group workout · small kindness for a neighbor · plan a weekend activity with someone.
Plan: top 3 priorities · time-block your day including a workout · plan 3 days of meals + grocery list · 10-min declutter · one 25-min focus session · weekly review (wins, lessons, goals) · lay out tomorrow's workout clothes · archive old email.
Rest: consistent bedtime, 7+ h · screens off 30–60 min before bed · 20-min nap or quiet rest · warm shower and gentle stretches · full rest day · 10 min foam rolling · book a check-up or dental visit · caffeine-free evening tea.
Create: sketch 15 min · healthy recipe from another cuisine · new workout playlist · short poem or story · 5 nature photos on your walk · 15 min learning a song, riff or dance.
